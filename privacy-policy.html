Welcome to Romantic Story. This Privacy Policy describes how Dubai Explore ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information in connection with your use of our mobile application Romantic Story (Package: kahaniya_app_in_hindi_offline.story_app_in_english.kahaniya_app.kahaniya_hindi_free).

Summary: Romantic Story is an entertainment and reading application. We respect your privacy: we do not require user accounts, we do not collect personal identities, and we do not store your reading habits on private company servers.

1. Account-Free Experience

Romantic Story does not require registration, login, or personal profile creation. You can use the app without providing your name, email address, phone number, or social media accounts.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

While our app does not collect personal identity information, our third-party advertising partners automatically receive standard device telemetry to serve advertisements and ensure app security:

  • Device Identifiers: Standard non-resettable or resettable identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID).
  • App Activity & Interactions: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, video views, and performance diagnostics.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry: Technical logs, app performance stats, and crash data to identify and resolve software errors.

3. Local On-Device Storage

To provide an enjoyable reading experience, certain preferences are stored strictly on your local device's sandboxed storage (such as your saved favorite stories, reading progress, and unlocked audio status). This data stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements (such as banners, interstitials, native cards, and rewarded ads) that help keep Romantic Story free. Google AdMob may collect and process information in accordance with its privacy standards:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can manage or reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device's Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.

5. Content Delivery Network

Story artwork and narration audio files are securely streamed over encrypted HTTPS protocols from our content delivery servers. When downloading media, your device communicates directly with the server to retrieve the file requested by the reader.

6. Data Encryption in Transit

All communication between the app and remote servers (including ad networks and media delivery) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences and adults. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal data of a minor has been inadvertently submitted, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not maintain central user accounts or cloud databases of user information, there are no remote user accounts to delete. You can delete all local application data, bookmarks, and cached files at any time by clearing the app's cache and storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.
